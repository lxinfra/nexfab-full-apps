About Us

NexoFab Industries Limited is a diversified infrastructure and manufacturing company headquartered in Indore, Madhya Pradesh. Incorporated in June 2025, we were founded on a simple belief — that a company should stand behind everything it builds, from the first drawing to years of after-sales service.

Our infrastructure division carries out road construction and builds government buildings such as schools and hospitals. Alongside it, we manufacture packaged drinking water and PET bottles, design and build machines — from photocopy machines to custom industrial equipment — and run a full repair & maintenance division.

In FY 2025-26, our first full financial year, NexoFab recorded a turnover of ₹40 crore. With a growing order book in roads and government buildings, our proposed turnover for FY 2026-27 is ₹250 crore.

Execution Methodology

Every NexoFab project follows a disciplined, step-by-step process — from the first look at the tender to the day we hand over the finished work.

01

Tender Study & Planning

Detailed study of tender documents and drawings, site survey, quantity estimation and a clear construction schedule.

02

Mobilisation

Site office set-up, deployment of machinery, skilled manpower and approved materials as per the work programme.

03

Earthwork & Foundations

Site clearance, excavation, embankment and sub-grade preparation for roads; foundations and plinth for buildings.

04

Construction

Road layers from sub-base to bituminous or concrete surface; RCC frame, masonry and services for buildings.

05

Quality Control & Testing

Material and workmanship testing at every stage, with inspections by the client’s engineers as per specifications.

06

Finishing & Handover

Finishing works, road markings and safety items, snag closure, documentation and timely handover to the client.
