Who We Are

Building Infrastructure, Producing & Repairing — Under One Roof

NexoFab Industries Limited is a diversified infrastructure and manufacturing company. We construct roads and government buildings such as schools and hospitals, produce packaged drinking water and PET bottles, design and build machines — from photocopy machines to custom industrial equipment — and provide complete repair and maintenance services.

  • Road construction & government building works
  • Packaged drinking water & PET bottle production
  • Design & manufacturing of office and industrial machines
  • Repair, redesign & annual maintenance services
  • Licensed, certified & quality-audited operations
