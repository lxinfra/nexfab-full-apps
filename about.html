Our Story

A Company That Builds Infrastructure, Produces & Repairs

NexoFab Industries Limited was founded on a simple belief — that a manufacturer should stand behind everything it makes, from the first design sketch to years of after-sales service.

Today we operate across four verticals: infrastructure & construction — road construction and government buildings such as schools and hospitals — high-volume manufacturing of packaged drinking water and PET bottles, the design and construction of machines — including photocopy machines and custom industrial equipment — and a full-fledged repair & maintenance division that services what we and others build.

In FY 2025-26 — our first full financial year — NexoFab recorded a turnover of ₹40 crore, and our proposed turnover for FY 2026-27 is ₹250 crore. Every road, building and product we deliver carries the NexoFab mark of quality — licensed, certified, and inspected without compromise.
