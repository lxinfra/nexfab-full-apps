Vertical 01

Packaged Water & PET Bottles

We manufacture plastic water bottles and packaged drinking water on fully automated lines — the same category trusted by leading national brands. From preform to filled, capped, and labeled bottle, every stage is controlled in-house.

  • PET bottle blowing & molding — multiple sizes (250 ml to 20 L)
  • Purified, mineral-enriched packaged drinking water
  • Automated filling, capping & labeling lines
  • Hygiene-first plant with batch-wise lab testing
  • Private-label / contract bottling for other brands
Vertical 02

Machine Manufacturing

We create and build machines — from office workhorses like photocopy machines to purpose-built industrial equipment. Our in-house team handles design, prototyping, fabrication, and assembly end-to-end.

  • Photocopy machines & office equipment
  • Custom industrial machinery, built to order
  • In-house design, CAD modeling & prototyping
  • Precision fabrication & quality-tested assembly
  • Installation, training & after-sales support
Vertical 03

Repair & Maintenance

A machine is only as good as the service behind it. Our repair division restores, redesigns, and maintains machines of every kind — whether we built them or someone else did.

  • Repair of photocopiers, office & industrial machines
  • Redesign & upgrading of older equipment
  • Annual Maintenance Contracts (AMC)
  • Genuine spare parts & skilled technicians
  • On-site service and rapid response turnaround
Vertical 04

Infrastructure & Construction

Our civil construction division builds the infrastructure communities rely on every day — roads that connect villages and towns, and government buildings such as schools and hospitals. We handle each project from site preparation to final handover.

  • Road construction — new roads, widening & strengthening
  • Government school buildings & educational campuses
  • Hospital & healthcare facility construction
  • Other public & government buildings
  • Quality control, safety compliance & on-time delivery
